Your fields

    1

    Map

    You state your inquiry and the studies you know. The tool bounds the field, reads the core papers, and lines up every prediction with the design of every test: data, sample, period, measures, estimator, controls, form, identification.

    Steps 0–4 · you confirm, edit, merge or send the tool back

    2

    Judge

    Claims are visible; assumptions are hidden. The tool unearths what the designs rest on and how sensitive each result is to it. You judge what is reasonable, questionable or violated, from your knowledge of the field.

    Step 5 · the expert's step, not a confirmation

    3

    Test

    Decide per prediction: a multiverse on existing data, or a new study with a different design. The decision opens Replication Navigator, which drafts reproduction code from the original paper and guides a preregistered replication. The result comes back into the map.

    Steps 6–7 and Replication Navigator
