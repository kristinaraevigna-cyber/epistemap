Your fields

    Welcome to your first step in seeing your field whole.

    Evidence Atlas is an instrument for meta-science. It reads the core papers of a field with you, lines up what each study predicted and how each test was designed, and shows where the evidence agrees, disagrees, and has never looked.

    The tool helps you with the following

    Map

    State your inquiry and the studies you know. The tool bounds the field, reads the core papers, and lines up every prediction with the design of every test.

    Judge

    Claims are visible; assumptions are hidden. The tool unearths what the designs rest on and how sensitive each result is. You judge what is reasonable, questionable, or violated.

    Test

    Decide per prediction: a multiverse on existing data, or a new study with a different design. The decision opens Replication Navigator, and the result comes back into the map.

    What is an evidence atlas?

    A map of a field at the level of its tests: which predictions recur, which designs were used to test them, where results disagree, and which assumptions no study has yet varied.
