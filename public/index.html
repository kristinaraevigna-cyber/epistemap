Coverage Map

An instrument for meta-research · empirical focus

Map what a field has tested, judge what it assumes, decide what to test next

You state your inquiry. The tool reads the field's core empirical studies, extracts the predictions they set out to test and the research design of every test, lines them up, and unearths the assumptions the designs rest on. You judge what is reasonable and decide what to test next: a multiverse on existing data, or a new study with a different design, planned in Replication Navigator. The tool proposes with a quote and page; you dispose.

    0

    Your inquiry

    You go first: what you want to understand, your search terms, the studies you already know, and what you think the field takes for granted.

    1

    Corpus

    Bound the field with OpenAlex or a Web of Science / Scopus export; seminal, recent and rising studies; reviews flagged. You decide which are core.

    2

    Studies

    Upload PDFs. The tool extracts each paper's hypotheses, research questions or propositions, the design of every test (per study), the result, and the analytical choices, with quotes and pages.

    3

    Predictions

    The tool proposes; you dispose: confirm, edit, merge or reject each prediction, add your own, and mark the core ones. Not satisfied? Send the tool back to the search.

    4

    Design map

    For each core prediction, the range of designs used across studies and the result under each: the design decisions that matter.

    5

    Assumptions & decision

    The tool unearths the hidden assumptions the designs rest on; you judge which are reasonable, then decide: a multiverse on existing data, or a new study with a different design.

    6

    Choice space

    The analytical choices found across papers, benchmarked against a published reference map (Berchicci & King, 2022 built in).

    7

    Reproduce, replicate, export

    Hand the chosen test to Replication Navigator (reproduction code, then a preregistered replication), and export the map for a multiverse.

    Papers are read once by the model and are not stored. Fields are saved on the server so a team can work on them together. Grounded in 322 verified evidence cards from the replication-methods literature. Built to feed Replication Navigator, which plans the test.
