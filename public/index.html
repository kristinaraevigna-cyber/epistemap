Coverage MapWhich assumptions of a claim has the evidence tested?

Map what a field's evidence has and has not tested

For each core claim in a field, Coverage Map breaks the claim into its assumptions (theory, constructs, design and sample, analysis, context), records which studies varied each one, and shows where the next test would be most informative. The AI reads papers and proposes; you confirm every item.

Your fields

    How it works. 1 Corpus: find the field's core studies (OpenAlex, or a Web of Science / Scopus export). 2 Studies: upload PDFs; the AI extracts claims, assumptions and analysis choices with page-referenced quotes. 3 Claims: confirm or edit the decomposition. 4 Coverage: tag which studies varied each assumption; see the map. 5 Choice space: compare AI-found analysis choices with a reference map (benchmark). 6 Export.

    Papers are read once by the model and not stored. Fields are saved on the server so a team can work on them together.
