Coverage Map

An instrument for meta-research

Which assumptions of a claim has the evidence tested?

Replications are counted. What they test is rarely examined. Coverage Map breaks each core claim of a field into its assumptions, reads the studies to record which assumptions each one varied, and shows where the next test would be most informative. The AI proposes with a quote and page; you confirm every item.

Your fields

    1

    Corpus

    Find the field's core studies with OpenAlex or a Web of Science / Scopus export. You decide which are core.

    2

    Studies

    Upload a PDF. The model extracts claims, their assumptions in five dimensions, and every analytical choice, each with a quote and page.

    3

    Claims

    Confirm, edit or reject each claim and assumption. Set how consequential each assumption is.

    4

    Coverage map

    Tag further studies against a claim. See which assumptions are covered, partly covered or untested.

    5

    Choice space

    Compare the analytical choices the AI found with a published reference map (Berchicci & King, 2022 is built in).

    6

    Export

    Diagnostic coverage per claim, the most informative next tests, JSON and CSV for analysis.

    Papers are read once by the model and are not stored. Fields are saved on the server so a team can work on them together. Grounded in 322 verified evidence cards from the replication-methods literature.
