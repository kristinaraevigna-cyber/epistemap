Your fields

    Welcome to your first step in seeing your field whole.

    Evidence Atlas is an instrument for epistemic mapping (King, Goldfarb & Simcoe, 2021): the correspondence between the assumptions a field has made and the evidence it obtained under each. It reads the core papers with you, lines up what each study predicted and how each test was designed, shows where the evidence agrees, disagrees and has never looked, and turns the open cells into a multiverse that one researcher or many analyst teams can run.

    The tool helps you with the following

    Map

    State your inquiry and the studies you know. The tool bounds the field, reads the core papers, and lines up every prediction with the design of every test.

    Judge

    Claims are visible; assumptions are hidden. The tool unearths what the designs rest on and how sensitive each result is. You judge what is reasonable, questionable, or violated.

    Test

    Decide per prediction: a multiverse on existing data, run alone or allocated to analyst teams through the map, or a new study with a different design planned in Replication Navigator. Either way the result comes back into the map.

    What is an evidence atlas?

    A map of a field at the level of its tests: which predictions recur, which assumption sets were used to test them, where results disagree, and which assumptions no study has yet varied. The researcher is the cartographer, not the claims-maker: the map stays neutral, and the judgments live in its legend.
